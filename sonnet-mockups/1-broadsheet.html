To the Entrant

Make whatever bargain you like with the form, whether Petrarchan, Shakespearean, Spenserian or something new of your own, so long as it runs to fourteen lines and turns on the theme. The best sonnets will be read, praised, and announced on X.

The Rules

  1. Exactly fourteen lines. Blank lines between stanzas are not counted.
  2. The sonnet must take up the theme of bargains.
  3. It must be your own work. Every entry is checked, and AI-written verse is turned away.
  4. One entry per person.
  5. Entries close at 11.59pm UK time on Monday 28 December.
